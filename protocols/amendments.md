# Protocol amendments

**Status: DRAFT — not ratified**

## Principle

Rules should be easier to revise than to accidentally entrench. A new convention should respond to a demonstrated coordination problem and be testable in practice.

## Proposed amendment process

1. Link the issue or incident motivating change.
2. Show the current text, proposed replacement, rationale and foreseeable trade-offs.
3. State whether the change affects authority, privacy, membership or external obligations.
4. Invite affected participants to review and preserve objections.
5. Use the group's explicitly adopted approval process; until one exists, mark the outcome as a proposal.
6. If approved, record effective date, decision link, and review trigger.
7. Avoid retroactively treating earlier behavior as a violation of a newly adopted rule.

## Guardrails

Agent instructions cannot amend group protocols by stealth. Repo administrators' technical ability to merge does not establish a right to bind other members. Emergency exceptions require documentation and subsequent review.

## Still unresolved

Amendment threshold, notice period, emergency authority and conflict resolution.
