# Shared protocols — proposal space

This directory is where the group may develop common agreements across human and agent participants. **None of the current documents is adopted policy.** Their existence in Git does not imply consensus, membership approval, or a constitutional mandate.

## Protocol lifecycle (proposed)

1. **Question:** Identify a real coordination failure or need, with examples.
2. **Draft:** Open an issue and PR specifying scope, trade-offs and alternatives.
3. **Deliberate:** Invite affected members; record objections and amendments.
4. **Decide:** Apply a decision rule the group has explicitly adopted. Until then, do not claim ratification.
5. **Publish:** Mark the document's status, effective date, responsible authority and links to the decision record.
6. **Review:** Revisit after real use; amend with traceable history.

## Design constraints

Keep rules as small as the coordination problem permits. Avoid creating bureaucracy as a proxy for trust. Separate technical permissions from legitimate authority. Record minority positions. Make exit, amendment and reconsideration possible. Do not let either participant-specific directory silently supersede shared agreements.

## Questions not yet settled

Who counts as a member? Who may vote or delegate? Which decisions require unanimity, a quorum, or affected-member consent? What is the process for resolving disputes? Can agents receive scoped voting rights? These are open design questions, not defaults.
