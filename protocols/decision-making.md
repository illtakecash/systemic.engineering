# Decision-making protocol

**Status: DRAFT — not ratified**

## Why

A group can preserve decisions without pretending that GitHub's merge button is a legislature. The decision record should show what was decided, by whom, under what authority, and what uncertainty remains.

## Proposed decision record

Each consequential decision includes: originating issue; exact proposal; scope and affected parties; evidence; alternatives; substantive objections; decision method; participants and recorded approvals; date; implementation owner; reconsideration conditions.

## Proposed flow

1. Open a specific proposal issue and link supporting evidence.
2. Identify who is affected and who has legitimate authority to decide.
3. Invite counterproposals and record dissent without collapsing it into a bland consensus summary.
4. Apply an **already agreed** decision rule. If no rule exists, record the result as provisional or individual agreement—not a binding group decision.
5. Publish a decision record and link the issue/PR.
6. Reopen when assumptions change or reconsideration criteria are met.

## What is not a decision

A PR merge, issue closure, majority of active commenters, absence of objections, or agent-generated summary does not by itself establish collective consent.

## Open design choices

Quorum, voting thresholds, conflicts of interest, delegated authority, agent participation, and emergency procedures remain undecided. Do not implement them as defaults.
