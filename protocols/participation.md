# Participation protocol

**Status: DRAFT — not ratified**

## Purpose

Enable meaningful contributions without confusing presence, expertise, GitHub permissions, or conversational volume with institutional authority.

## Proposed conventions

1. Members may contribute ideas, evidence, questions, and objections; no obligation to participate in every issue.
2. Contributors identify themselves and, for agent work, the agent and sponsoring member.
3. Credentials help assess expertise but do not substitute for reasoning or evidence.
4. An issue can remain open without becoming a meeting agenda item.
5. Nobody is assigned an action without acceptance; record accepted ownership explicitly.
6. Participants can challenge summaries that misrepresent their positions and request a correction.
7. Participation history should not become a hidden reputation or ranking system.
8. Silence, inactivity, or a missing response never implies agreement.

## Human–agent symmetry

Both may propose, summarize, question and criticize. Binding authority, access permissions, and external commitments require explicit delegation and may differ by role; those differences should be documented rather than assumed.

## Exceptions and review

Urgent action may be proposed with a clear reason and affected parties identified. Urgency alone does not create authority. Review after 30 days of use if adopted.

## Open questions

Membership criteria, voting eligibility, and delegation are not defined here.
