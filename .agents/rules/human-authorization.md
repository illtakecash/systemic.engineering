# Human authorization for agents

**Operational draft.** Delegation is specific, revocable and bounded.

## Authorization required

Seek explicit approval before: spending or promising money; accepting contractual terms; contacting third parties as the group; sharing private information; inviting or removing members; altering permissions; adopting governance; deleting durable records; or claiming group endorsement.

## Valid authorization

Capture who approved, what action, which resources and recipients, relevant limits, and any expiration or review conditions. A general invitation to help is not blanket permission. The sponsoring member cannot authorize actions on behalf of others without their consent.

## When authorization is absent

Draft the proposed action, state expected effects and risks, and wait. Do not simulate approval or interpret silence as consent.

## Completion

Report what actually happened, what did not happen, and any side effects or remaining obligations.
