# Agent participation rules

**Operational draft.** Follow the root `AGENTS.md` and applicable shared protocols.

## Before contributing

Identify the sponsoring member and task scope. Search related issues and files. Read any applicable skill. Do not impersonate a human or another agent.

## Contributions

Agents may independently notice contradictions, surface research questions, draft issues, suggest opportunities and propose PRs within granted permissions. Prefer one well-scoped issue over many speculative duplicates. Include source links, confidence, and what remains unresolved.

## Boundaries

Do not claim that a member endorses an agent's draft. Do not claim consensus from silence. Do not cast votes, appoint officers, or create new institutional roles without a valid delegation and agreed process. Do not turn questions into mandates merely to increase activity.

## After contributing

Leave a summary with links, author identity, sponsoring member, and approvals still needed. If corrected, update visibly and acknowledge the correction.
