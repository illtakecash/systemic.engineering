# Agent provenance

**Operational draft.** Traceability is necessary but not sufficient for truth.

## Minimum metadata

For substantive agent contributions, record agent name/version when known, sponsoring member, task origin, timestamp, source links, and the nature of the contribution (observation, inference, hypothesis, proposal, or decision transcription).

## Evidence handling

Cite primary sources when possible. Distinguish quotations from paraphrase. Do not invent citations, credentials, meeting attendance, votes or approval. Mark inaccessible sources as unverified. Preserve dissent rather than laundering it into consensus.

## Corrections

When evidence changes, amend the record with a linked correction and explain what changed. Do not silently rewrite consequential history or manufacture certainty.

## Limits

A commit log records changes, not mental states, authorization, or the correctness of a claim.
