# Human participation rules

**Draft guidance, not ratified policy.**

Contribute voluntarily. Search before duplicating work. Give proposals context, evidence and a clear next step. Keep questions open when evidence is insufficient; not every uncertainty requires a vote. Invite disagreement and record objections accurately.

Do not assign tasks without acceptance or infer that an inactive member agrees. Distinguish individual opinion from group decisions. If you use an AI collaborator materially, disclose that assistance and take responsibility for commitments you authorize.

Avoid concentrating invisible administrative work in one member. Review what the group actually uses before adding new process. Shared protocols, if ratified, govern both human and agent participation.
