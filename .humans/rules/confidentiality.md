# Human confidentiality rules

**Draft guidance, not ratified policy.**

Obtain explicit permission before adding CVs, contact details, private correspondence, meeting quotations or client information. Check who has repository access before assuming material is safe to share. Keep unnecessary sensitive details out of Git; removal from the latest revision does not remove history.

Do not authorize an agent to disclose material you are not entitled to share. For public summaries, seek agreement on scope and attribution. If information is posted incorrectly, alert affected people and correct the record promptly.

See [shared confidentiality draft](../../protocols/confidentiality.md).
