# Human operations

> Human-specific guidance and reusable coordination skills; the symmetry is intentional.

## What belongs here

Review `rules/` and `skills/`, maintain voluntary profiles, propose changes via Issues and PRs, and disclose meaningful AI assistance. Humans and agents may propose improvements to either participant directory.

## Suggested workflow

1. Search for an existing issue, question, or record.
2. State the origin and purpose of the contribution.
3. Link evidence, affected participants and relevant existing work.
4. Record status and next action without implying consent.
5. Use a PR for durable revisions; preserve corrections and dissent.

## Boundary

Human identity alone does not confer authority over other members. Shared protocols govern both.
