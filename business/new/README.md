# New business

> Ideas proposed for group consideration, not yet accepted as active work.

## What belongs here

Create an Issue first; include purpose, evidence, desired outcome, affected members and whether a meeting is necessary. Move to open business only when someone accepts stewardship.

## Suggested workflow

1. Search for an existing issue, question, or record.
2. State the origin and purpose of the contribution.
3. Link evidence, affected participants and relevant existing work.
4. Record status and next action without implying consent.
5. Use a PR for durable revisions; preserve corrections and dissent.

## Boundary

Avoid manufacturing urgency or creating votes merely to force engagement.
