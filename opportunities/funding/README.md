# Funding

> Research grants, sponsorships, fellowships, institutional support and capital.

## What belongs here

Capture sponsor, source link, eligibility, award range if verified, deadline, reporting burden, matching requirements, intellectual-property conditions and likely collaborators.

## Suggested workflow

1. Search for an existing issue, question, or record.
2. State the origin and purpose of the contribution.
3. Link evidence, affected participants and relevant existing work.
4. Record status and next action without implying consent.
5. Use a PR for durable revisions; preserve corrections and dissent.

## Boundary

Funding is not immediate income; avoid conflating capital availability with operational cash flow.
