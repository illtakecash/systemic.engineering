# Consulting and immediate income

> Time-sensitive paid work, referrals and collaborative delivery.

## What belongs here

Capture client category (anonymize when needed), required skills, timing, scope, budget if disclosed, conflicts, lead owner and next step. Clarify who contracts, invoices and owns deliverables before committing.

## Suggested workflow

1. Search for an existing issue, question, or record.
2. State the origin and purpose of the contribution.
3. Link evidence, affected participants and relevant existing work.
4. Record status and next action without implying consent.
5. Use a PR for durable revisions; preserve corrections and dissent.

## Boundary

An opportunity is not a group contract; each member chooses whether to participate.
